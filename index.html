La Vie — The Art
of Celebrating Life.

Embraced by lush greenery and the quiet pulse of Boudha, Lavie Garden is our love letter to slow living — a restaurant, a garden, and a boutique stay gathered under the same open sky. The name, drawn from the French la vie, simply means the life: and here, life is meant to be savoured.

Our chefs compose an international table threaded with Nepali soul — Pomegranate Pani Puri shots, Pulled Pork Benedict, hand-stretched pizza, Dudh Malai Cake — paired with crafted cocktails and cold brews pulled beneath a canopy of leaves. At dusk, the lanterns warm, the bonfire lights, and the garden begins to sing.

“From morning coffee to evening cocktails under the stars.”
